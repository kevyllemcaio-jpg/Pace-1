# GUERRA DE ANIMES: prompts para IA de vídeo (Kling, Veo, Hailuo, Runway)

## Como usar
1. **Primeiro gere as 4 imagens de personagem** (ficha abaixo) numa IA de imagem ou na própria IA de vídeo, com fundo neutro e corpo inteiro. Escolha a melhor de cada um.
2. **Em cada cena, use a imagem do personagem como referência** ("image to video" ou "character reference") e cole o prompt da cena. Assim eles não mudam de cara.
3. Gere cada cena em **9:16, de 5 a 8 segundos**. Gere 2 ou 3 versões de cada e fique com a melhor.
4. **Me mande os arquivos na ordem (cena 1, 2, 3…).** Eu monto com cortes no ritmo, efeitos sonoros de cada golpe, trilha épica e o final.

## Estilo (cole no começo de TODA cena)
```
High-end anime film style, cinematic 3D-anime hybrid like a modern anime feature, sharp clean linework feel, dramatic cinematic lighting with strong rim light and glow, vibrant saturated colors, dynamic camera, smooth fluid motion, speed lines and energy effects, epic atmosphere, vertical 9:16, no text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermark.
```
**Negativo (se a IA tiver o campo):**
```
text, subtitles, letters, logo, watermark, blurry, low quality, deformed hands, extra fingers, extra limbs, distorted face, morphing, flickering, jittery motion, character changing appearance, horizontal frame
```

## Ficha dos personagens (repita a mesma descrição sempre que ele aparecer)
- **KAITO (herói, fogo):** `a young male anime fighter with spiky bright red hair, intense orange eyes, sleeveless orange martial arts gi with black belt, muscular arms, black wristbands, fiery orange aura`
- **REN (ninja, raio):** `a young male anime ninja with long white hair in a ponytail, sharp blue eyes, dark navy ninja outfit, long flowing blue scarf, crackling blue lightning aura`
- **MIKA (espadachim):** `a young female anime swordswoman with long purple hair, violet eyes, white short kimono top with purple sash, dark purple pants, holding a katana with a glowing pink energy blade`
- **DRAVEN (vilão):** `a tall menacing anime villain with messy black hair, glowing red eyes, a scar over one eye, pale skin, dark spiked armor with small horns, long torn dark crimson cape, dark purple aura`

## Cenas
**Cena 1: encarada (6 s)**
```
[ESTILO] KAITO and DRAVEN stand face to face in a ruined stone arena at sunset, ten meters apart, wind blowing dust and small rocks trembling on the ground, Kaito in fighting stance, Draven smirking with arms crossed, orange and purple sky with clouds. Camera: slow push-in from a low wide angle.
```
**Cena 2: close do Kaito (4 s)**
```
[ESTILO] Extreme close-up of KAITO's face, eyes narrowing with determination, red hair blowing in the wind, orange aura flickering around him, sweat drop. Camera: slow push-in on the eyes.
```
**Cena 3: close do Draven (4 s)**
```
[ESTILO] Extreme close-up of DRAVEN smiling menacingly, red eyes glowing, dark purple aura rising, cape flowing behind him. Camera: slow tilt up from the mouth to the eyes.
```
**Cena 4: choque de socos (6 s)**
```
[ESTILO] KAITO and DRAVEN dash toward each other at super speed and their fists collide in mid-air, a massive shockwave ring explodes outward, cracking the stone floor, dust and debris flying, bright flash at the impact. Camera: side view, slow motion at the moment of impact, then camera shake.
```
**Cena 5: troca de golpes (6 s)**
```
[ESTILO] KAITO unleashes a rapid barrage of punches and kicks on DRAVEN, who blocks with his forearms, motion blur and speed lines, orange sparks on each hit. Camera: fast dynamic tracking around them.
```
**Cena 6: Ren entra no raio (6 s)**
```
[ESTILO] REN appears in a flash of blue lightning and delivers a flying kick to DRAVEN's chest, sending him sliding back across the arena, lightning sparks trailing, KAITO smiles. Camera: low angle, the kick comes toward the camera.
```
**Cena 7: transformação do vilão (6 s)**
```
[ESTILO] DRAVEN screams as a giant dark purple aura erupts around him, the ground cracks, rocks float upward into the air, his eyes glow intensely red, cape whipping violently. Camera: low angle slow orbit, dramatic.
```
**Cena 8: corte da Mika (6 s)**
```
[ESTILO] MIKA leaps in and slashes her glowing pink katana at DRAVEN, leaving a huge pink crescent energy arc in the air, Draven blocks with his armored arm, pink sparks explode. Camera: slow motion during the slash, then normal speed.
```
**Cena 9: disputa de rajadas (8 s)**
```
[ESTILO] KAITO fires a huge orange fire energy beam from his hands, DRAVEN fires a dark purple energy beam, the two beams collide in the middle of the arena creating a giant glowing sphere of energy, both fighters straining, ground breaking beneath them. Camera: slow 180-degree orbit around the clash.
```
**Cena 10: os três juntos (6 s)**
```
[ESTILO] REN and MIKA place their hands on KAITO's back, their blue and pink auras merge with his orange aura, the orange beam grows enormous and overpowers the purple beam, pushing it back toward DRAVEN. Camera: behind the three heroes, epic wide shot.
```
**Cena 11: explosão (5 s)**
```
[ESTILO] A gigantic explosion of orange light engulfs DRAVEN, a massive dome of energy and smoke expands across the arena, debris flying, blinding flash. Camera: wide shot with heavy camera shake.
```
**Cena 12: vitória (6 s)**
```
[ESTILO] The smoke clears at sunset, KAITO, REN and MIKA stand together tired but smiling, DRAVEN lies defeated on the cracked ground in the background, embers floating in the air. Camera: slow pull-back and rise into a wide epic shot.
```
